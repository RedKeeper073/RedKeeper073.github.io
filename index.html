Personal-use application

FinLab Export

A private-use tool for sending selected research reports from the owner's server to the owner's Google Drive for independent review.

Purpose

FinLab Export uses rclone to transfer specifically approved synthetic or anonymized research reports. Source files remain on the owner's server; permitted copies are stored privately in the owner's Google Drive.

It is not a public file-sharing, banking, brokerage, or automated trading service.

Google Drive permissions

FinLab Export requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This limits application access to files it creates or that the owner explicitly makes available to it, rather than the owner's full Drive.

Project rules exclude passwords, OAuth tokens, account identifiers, raw bank statements, and real trading instructions from report exports.

Privacy and independent review

Approved reports in Google Drive can be accessed through the owner's separately authorized ChatGPT–Google Drive connection for independent analysis. The content is processed subject to the relevant service's terms and privacy controls. The reports are not published on this website.

Read the Privacy Policy for details about data handling, retention, and revoking access.
