Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026. This policy covers FinLab Export, a personal-use Google Drive integration operated by the owner of this repository. It is not a public service.

Purpose and types of data

FinLab Export transfers selected, owner-approved synthetic or anonymized research reports from the owner's private server to the owner's Google Drive. Approved reports may include research identifiers, model portfolio amounts, calculations, assumptions and findings. Financial account numbers, passwords, OAuth tokens, full bank statements and personal identifiers are excluded from permitted report exports by project policy.

Google API access

FinLab Export requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This grants access to files created by or explicitly made available to this application, not unrestricted access to the owner's entire Google Drive. The owner can revoke access in their Google Account settings.

Storage and sharing

Source material remains on the owner's private server. Selected exports are held in the owner's Google Drive and are not made public by this website. Access tokens and client configuration are stored on the private server, not in this repository.

For independent review, the owner may access selected Google Drive reports via a separately authorized ChatGPT–Google Drive connection. In such cases, report content is processed through that service in accordance with its applicable terms and privacy settings. FinLab Export does not sell information to advertisers.

Retention and removal

The owner controls local report retention and removal of exported files from Drive. Deleting a file from Drive may not immediately remove copies from Trash, backups or provider systems. The owner may revoke FinLab Export permissions in Google Account settings and invalidate or remove server-side credentials.

Website visitors

This static site has no forms, advertising scripts or custom analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about this personal project may be directed to the owner through the repository issue tracker. Please do not post financial records or security credentials in public issues.

Changes

This policy will be updated to reflect material changes to the integration's actual data handling.
